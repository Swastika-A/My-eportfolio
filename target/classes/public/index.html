About Me

I'm a software developer who enjoys turning ideas into reliable, working applications. I'm most drawn to the backend: the logic, data and structure that the rest of an application depends on. I see it the same way I see life — the foundations people don't always notice are what allow everything else to succeed, so they are worth building carefully.

I'm in the final semester of a Bachelor of Software Engineering at The University of the South Pacific. My internship as a Plant Logistics Intern at Natural Waters of Viti Ltd showed me how technology supports real operations. I worked with standard operating procedure (SOP) documentation, used an Oracle database in a workplace setting and was exposed to the technology used on the production side of the business.

I like learning new programming languages and technologies, and I aim to understand them properly rather than just enough to get by. Going forward, I want to keep growing in backend development, database-driven systems and responsive web applications.

“In order to break limitations, one has to cross the extents of imagination.”

— A thought I try to work by
